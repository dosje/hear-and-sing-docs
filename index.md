# Hear & Sing

**Hear & Sing** is a native iOS audio player for importing and playing your own audio files — music, sermons, or Bible recordings from JW.org. No account required, no subscription, no external API. Fully offline after download.

## What does the app do?

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| [Library](docs/features#library-tab) | Import and manage local audio files |
| [Browse](docs/features#browse-tab) | Browse JW.org and add audio to your library |
| [Playlists](docs/features#playlists-tab) | Create and play custom playlists |
| [Search](docs/features#search-tab) | Find any track by title, artist, or album |
| [Settings](docs/features#settings-tab) | Storage management, Siri shortcuts, and more |

## Key highlights

- **Local audio player** — import MP3, M4A, AAC, OPUS, FLAC files; legally equivalent to VLC
- **JW.org browser** — built-in browser detects audio pages and offers one-tap import
- **Bible audio** — browse the New World Translation by book and chapter in any language
- **CarPlay** — full CarPlay support with Now Playing and list browsing
- **Siri Shortcuts** — play by song name, Bible book, JW Broadcasting episode, or shuffle your library by voice
- **iPhone & iPad** — fully adaptive layout for all screen sizes
- **Free** — no in-app purchases, no subscription
