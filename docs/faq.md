# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Is Hear & Sing free?**  
Yes. Hear & Sing is completely free — no in-app purchases and no subscription.

**Does the app require an account?**  
No. There is no account, login, or sign-up. All your data stays on your device.

**What iOS version is required?**  
iOS 18.0 or later on iPhone or iPad.

**What audio formats are supported?**  
MP3, M4A, AAC, OPUS, and FLAC.

---

## Importing Audio

**How do I add audio files from JW.org?**  
Open the Browse tab. The built-in browser navigates to jw.org. When you reach a music or audio page, an "Add to App" banner appears at the bottom. Tap it, select the tracks you want, and optionally choose to download them immediately.

**How do I import a file I already have on my device?**  
Open the Library tab and tap the **+** button. This opens a Files.app document picker. Select your audio file and it will be imported into Hear & Sing.

**Can I import from other apps like JW Library?**  
Yes. In JW Library (or any app), tap the Share button on an audio file and choose **Hear & Sing** from the share sheet. The file is copied to your library in the background.

## Downloads

**What does the cloud icon mean on a track?**  
A cloud icon means the audio has been added to your library but not yet downloaded to your device. Tap the icon to download it, or long-press the track and choose **Download**.

**Can I download entire albums at once?**  
Yes. In the Library tab, select multiple tracks by long-pressing and tapping additional items, then tap the **Download** button in the toolbar.

**Do downloads continue when I leave the app?**  
Yes. Hear & Sing uses iOS background URL sessions so downloads continue even when the app is in the background or the screen is locked.

---

## Playback

**Can I control playback from the lock screen?**  
Yes. Hear & Sing registers with MPNowPlayingInfoCenter and MPRemoteCommandCenter, so lock-screen controls, Control Center, and AirPods gestures all work.

**Does CarPlay work?**  
Yes. Connect your iPhone to a CarPlay-compatible system and Hear & Sing will appear in the CarPlay dashboard with a Now Playing screen and a library browsing list.

**How do I change the playback speed?**  
Open the full-screen player by tapping the Now Playing bar. The speed button (showing 1×) is in the player controls. Tap it to cycle through 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, and 2×.

## Siri & Shortcuts

**What Siri phrases work out of the box?**  
- "Play in Hear & Sing"
- "Shuffle my library in Hear & Sing"
- "Play [song name] in Hear & Sing"
- "Play [Bible book] in Hear & Sing"
- "Read [Bible book] in Hear & Sing"

**Can I create custom Siri phrases?**  
Yes. Go to Settings → Siri & Shortcuts and tap **Add Custom Phrase**. This opens the Shortcuts app where you can record a personal phrase that triggers any Hear & Sing action.

---

## Storage & Data

**How do I see how much storage the app is using?**  
Open the Settings tab. The Storage section shows the number of downloaded tracks, the number of library tracks, and total disk usage.

**How do I free up space without losing my library?**  
Go to Settings → Storage → **Delete All Downloads**. This removes the downloaded audio files but keeps the track entries in your library. You can re-download any track at any time.
