# Getting Started

Welcome to Hear & Sing! This guide walks you through setup and your first import so you can start listening right away.

## Requirements

- **iPhone or iPad** running iOS 18.0 or later
- No account or internet connection required after files are downloaded

## Step 1 — Install the app

Download Hear & Sing from the App Store. See the [Installation guide](../installation/) for details.

## Step 2 — Import your first audio file

There are three ways to add audio to your library:

### Option A — Share Sheet (from Safari or JW Library)

1. In Safari, navigate to a JW.org music or audio page
2. The app's built-in browser (Browse tab) will show an **"Add to App"** banner automatically
3. Select which tracks you want, optionally tick **Download now**, and tap **Add**

### Option B — Files.app document picker

1. Open the **Library tab**
2. Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner
3. Choose a file (MP3, M4A, AAC, OPUS, or FLAC) from Files.app
4. The track appears in your library immediately

### Option C — Share Extension (from any other app)

1. In any app (e.g. JW Library), tap the Share button on an audio file
2. Choose **Hear & Sing** from the share sheet
3. The file is copied to the app in the background

## Step 3 — Play a track

1. Open the **Library tab**
2. Tap any track to begin playback
3. The **Now Playing bar** appears at the bottom of every tab
4. Tap the bar to open the full-screen player with seek slider, speed control, and queue

## Step 4 — Download for offline use

Tracks added from JW.org via the Browse tab may not be downloaded yet. A cloud icon indicates a track is not yet saved locally.

- Tap the cloud icon on an individual track to download it
- Long-press a track and choose **Download** to queue it
- In the Library tab, select multiple tracks and tap **Download** for batch download

## Step 5 — Explore Bible audio

1. Open the **Library tab** and scroll to the **Bible** section (or tap the Bible chip)
2. Select a book from the 3-column grid
3. Select a chapter from the 4-column grid
4. Tap a chapter to play, or tap **Download All** to save the whole book
