# Installation

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running **iOS 18.0 or later**
- An Apple ID to download from the App Store

## Install from the App Store

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad
2. Search for **"Hear & Sing"**
3. Tap **Get** and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
4. The app installs and appears on your Home Screen

## Update the app

Updates are delivered through the App Store.

- Open the **App Store** and tap your profile picture in the top-right corner
- Scroll to **Available Updates** and tap **Update** next to Hear & Sing
- Or enable **Automatic Updates** in iOS Settings → App Store → App Updates

## Permissions

Hear & Sing requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Background App Refresh** | To continue downloading audio files when the app is in the background |
| **Siri & Suggestions** | To enable Siri Shortcuts for hands-free playback |
| **Local Network** | To enable AirPlay streaming to speakers and Apple TV |

No microphone, camera, contacts, or location access is requested.

## Uninstall

To remove the app and all downloaded files:

1. Long-press the Hear & Sing icon on your Home Screen
2. Tap **Remove App** → **Delete App**

All downloaded audio and library data stored in the app's container will be deleted.
