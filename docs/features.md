# Features

## Library Tab

The Library tab is the home for all your local audio files.

- Import audio files (MP3, M4A, AAC, OPUS, FLAC) via Share Sheet or Files.app document picker
- Browse tracks by **All**, **Albums**, and **Artists**
- Multi-select mode for batch **Download**, **Delete**, and **Add to Playlist**
- Tracks display artwork, title, artist, and album
- Edit track metadata (title, artist, album, category) with the Track Edit sheet
- Download individual tracks or entire albums with one tap

## Browse Tab

The Browse tab is a built-in web browser that makes adding JW.org audio simple.

- Navigates to jw.org automatically on first open
- An **"Add to App"** banner appears when you land on a JW.org music or audio page
- Select individual tracks or all tracks on the page
- Optionally tick **Download now** to queue downloads immediately
- Bible audio content is added to your library silently without a selection sheet
- A language filter bar appears when a page has tracks in multiple languages

## Bible Audio

Bible audio is accessible directly from the Library tab.

- Browse the New World Translation by book and chapter
- 3-column book grid; 4-column chapter grid
- The title shows the edition year: NWT (2013) for English, NWT (2018) for German, etc.
- Language selector chip bar when Bible tracks in multiple languages are present
- Book names are derived dynamically from track metadata — the correct language is always shown
- A **Download All** button is available per chapter view to save all chapters of a book
- Chapter cells show a cloud icon when the audio is not yet downloaded (iPad: icon on the right; iPhone: icon below the chapter number)

## Playlists Tab

- Create, rename, and delete playlists
- Add or remove individual tracks
- Play all tracks in a playlist or shuffle them

## Search Tab

- Full-text search across all library tracks
- Searches title, artist, and album simultaneously
- Results update live as you type

## Settings Tab

- **Haptic feedback** toggle
- **Siri & Shortcuts**: built-in phrases listed + "Add Custom Phrase" deep-link opens Shortcuts app
- **Storage**: Downloaded Tracks count, Library Tracks count, and total disk usage
- **Delete All Downloads** option to free up space without removing tracks from the library
- **Version** display

## Now Playing

- **Mini bar** at the bottom of every tab (iPhone); **sidebar panel** on iPad
- Full-screen player sheet with artwork, seek slider, prev/play/next, shuffle, speed control (0.75×–2×), and AirPlay picker
- Swipe the mini bar left or right to skip to the previous or next track
- **Up Next queue** visible in the full player
- **CarPlay** support: CPNowPlayingTemplate for the current track and CPListTemplate for library browsing

## Siri Shortcuts (AppIntents)

Hear & Sing includes several built-in Siri phrases:

- "Play in Hear & Sing"
- "Shuffle my library in Hear & Sing"
- "Play [song] in Hear & Sing"
- "Play [Bible book] in Hear & Sing"
- "Read [Bible book] in Hear & Sing"

Custom phrases can be created via the Shortcuts app using the **Add Custom Phrase** button in Settings.
